## Explore: a moment for a natural 20 or natural 1

Today a nat 20 or nat 1 only gets a `NATURAL 20` / `NATURAL 1` line under the total. Explore something with more
character when the d20 that counts lands on one, in the roll animation's style (flat pixel outlines, greyscale,
whole-number scales; see `HANDOFF.md` and `data/dice/`). Ideas to try:

- Art: a small pixel illustration or MEK Dings ornament, a Jacquard banner ("Critical!", "Fumble"), a border or
  flourish round the result.
- Motion: the die pulsing, ringing or cracking on landing, a burst of lines, the outline thickening, a shake on a 1.
- Haptics: a distinct pattern for each, beyond the usual landing tick.

Keep it calm, as Light tools are: short, never in the way, skippable with a tap, and off when the dice animation is.
Decide what happens with advantage (the dropped d20 doesn't count) and on a d20 with a modifier.
